# Governance

Open Risk Workbench is maintainer-led during its initial incubation.

Maintainers are responsible for:

- reviewing provenance and licensing;
- keeping the knowledge/execution seam explicit;
- protecting failure-closed behavior;
- triaging issues and security reports;
- publishing versioned releases and migration notes.

Material changes to schemas, evidence states, graph hashing, execution semantics, or production-authority labels require a documented design discussion before merge.

No contribution gains production authority merely by being merged. Production use requires an independent owner, controls, validation, and regulatory review outside this project.
